# MHFW-01 — Virtual Commissioning Rev A

Independent desktop horizontal flow wrapper research prototype, Windows, zero purchase cost.
Initial product 60 × 40 × 15 mm, bag pitch 120 mm, 5–10 packs/min.
The public Fujikikai product catalogue informs the functional stages only. No company drawings,
internal specifications, branding assets or proprietary code are used.

Rev A scope: executable IEC ST, native virtual PLC, loopback I/O, deterministic kinematic
machine model, browser HMI, normal and injected-fault integration tests.
Thermal sealing, food contact, actual film mechanics and physical safety remain future work.
Complete this phase and stop; do not automatically commission hardware or start Rev B.
